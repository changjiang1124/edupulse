Clock in when you arrive at the campus and clock out before you leave. Use your phone. You must be at the campus for both, because EduPulse checks your location each time.

Before your first shift

  1. On your phone, go to https://edupulse.perthartschool.com.au and log in with the username and password the office gave you.

  2. Open the clock page: tap the menu button at the top right, tap your name, then tap Clock In/Out.

  3. Save the clock page to your home screen so you can open it with one tap next time.

    • iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
    • Android (Chrome): tap the three dots at the top right, then Add to Home screen.

Clock in

  1. When you arrive, open the clock page. If your phone asks to use your location, tap Allow.

  2. Wait a few seconds. When the campus name and Verified appear, your location has been checked.

  3. Tick the class you are teaching today. This is optional. If you do not tick a class, EduPulse asks you to confirm and records general work.

  4. Tap Clock In.

  5. The page reloads and shows Currently Clocked In. You are done.

Clock out

  1. Before you leave the campus, open the clock page again and wait for Verified.

  2. Tap Clock Out. The page reloads and shows Currently Clocked Out.

If you forgot to clock out

If you do not clock out within 12 hours, that shift is not counted. Next time you open the clock page, you will see Missed Clock Out.

  • Clock in for today as normal.
  • Tell the office what time you finished that day. They will add it so you are paid correctly.

If something goes wrong

What you see What to do
Location is blocked for this website

Your phone is not sharing your location with the browser.

iPhone: open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Location Services. Turn Location Services on. Tap Safari Websites and choose While Using the App.

Android: turn on Location in your phone settings. In Chrome, tap the icon to the left of the website address, tap Permissions, and allow Location.

Then go back to the clock page and tap Try again.
Too far from a campus

Your phone thinks you are not at the campus. Go inside the building, turn on Wi-Fi (it makes the location more accurate) and tap Try again.

If it names a different campus from the one you are at, tell the office. That campus has not been set up yet.
No internet connection Check your mobile data or Wi-Fi, then tap the button again. Nothing was recorded.
You are already clocked in Your clock-in was already saved. Close the page and open it again. You will see the Clock Out button.
No facilities found with GPS coordinates No campus has been set up for clock-in yet. Tell the office.
