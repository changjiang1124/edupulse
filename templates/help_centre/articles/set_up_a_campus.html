When a teacher clocks in or out, EduPulse checks that their phone is at a campus. It compares the location of the phone with the location saved for each campus. You only need to set this up once for each campus.

Do this before teachers start clocking in. If a campus has no location saved, teachers cannot clock in there.

Save the campus location

  1. Click Facilities in the top menu. Click Edit next to the campus.

  2. Find the campus coordinates. On a computer, open Google Maps and find the campus building. Right-click on the building. The first line of the menu shows two numbers, for example -31.98161, 115.78838. Click the numbers to copy them.

  3. Paste the first number into GPS Latitude and the second number into GPS Longitude. The first number for Perth always starts with a minus sign.

  4. Check GPS Verification Radius (meters). This is how far from the saved point a teacher can be and still clock in. We recommend 100. Phone location inside a building is often 20 to 50 metres out, so a smaller number can stop teachers who are already inside.

    Campuses set up before September 2026 may still show 50. Change it to 100.

  5. Click Update Facility.

Check it works

Stand inside the campus with your phone and open Clock In/Out (see the next guide). The page should show the campus name and Verified. You do not need to press Clock In to check.

If the page says you are too far away while you are inside, check that the two numbers were not swapped. If they are right, try a radius of 150.
