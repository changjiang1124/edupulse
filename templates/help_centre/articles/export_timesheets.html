Timesheets download as Excel files. Hours are saved as numbers, so you can add them up in Excel.

For administrators: total hours for all staff

  1. Click Staff, then Timesheet Overview.

  2. Choose the pay period with From Date and To Date, then click Apply Filter.

  3. Click Export Summary. The file lists each person's total hours, working days and shifts for that period.

For administrators: every shift for one teacher

Use this when you need the clock-in and clock-out times, not just the totals.

  1. In the Timesheet Overview table, click the green Excel button next to the teacher. You can also click Excel in the Timesheet section of the teacher's page.

  2. The file has one row per shift: date, clock-in and clock-out times, class, campus and hours. It also shows whether each time came from the clock page (GPS Clock) or was added by the office (Manual Entry), and any audit note.

Before you export for payroll, check for shifts marked Missing clock-out and fix them first. Those hours are not included until a finish time is added. See Check and correct hours.

For teachers: your own hours

  1. Tap the menu button, tap your name, then tap My Timesheet.

  2. Choose the Start Date and End Date and tap Filter. Your total hours and each shift appear below.

  3. To download them, tap Export Current Range.

  4. Check the dates, then tap Export Timesheet. The Excel file downloads to your phone.

Teachers can only see and export their own hours.
