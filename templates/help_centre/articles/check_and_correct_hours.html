Each clock-in and clock-out makes one shift. Check the shifts before you run payroll, and fix any that are wrong or missing.

See everyone's hours

  1. Click Staff in the top menu, then click Timesheet Overview.

  2. Choose the dates. Click a quick range such as This Week, or pick From Date and To Date. To see one person only, choose them in Staff Member. Then click Apply Filter.

  3. The table shows each person's total hours, the number of days they worked and the number of shifts.

Check one teacher's shifts

  1. In the overview table, click the view button (the eye) next to the teacher. You can also open the teacher from the staff list.

  2. The Timesheet section lists every shift with the date, clock-in and clock-out times, class, campus and hours.

    Two labels need your attention:

    • In Progress means the teacher is clocked in right now. No action is needed.
    • Missing clock-out means the teacher did not clock out within 12 hours. These hours are not counted until you add the finish time.

Fix a missing clock-out or a wrong time

  1. Ask the teacher what time they finished. Then click Edit on that shift.

  2. Set Entry Mode to Full Session. Check the Work Date and Clock In Time, and enter the Clock Out Time.

  3. Click Optional Details and write a short Audit Note, for example "Forgot to clock out. Finish time confirmed by the teacher." This note appears in the Excel export.

  4. Click Save Changes. The shift now shows both times and its hours are counted.

Add a shift that was never recorded

Use this when a teacher worked but did not clock in at all, for example when their phone was flat.

  1. Open the teacher's page and click Add Entry in the Timesheet section.

  2. Leave Entry Mode as Full Session. Enter the Work Date, Clock In Time and Clock Out Time. Add an Audit Note under Optional Details.

  3. Click Save Timesheet Entry.

Shifts you add or edit are marked Manual Entry in the Excel export. Shifts recorded on the clock page are marked GPS Clock. This lets you see which hours were checked by location.
