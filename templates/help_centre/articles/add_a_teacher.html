Every teacher needs their own EduPulse login. They use it to clock in and out and to see their own hours.

Create the account

  1. Click Staff in the top menu, then click Add New Staff Member.

  2. Fill in the form:

    • Username is what the teacher types to log in. A simple pattern such as firstname.lastname works well.
    • Password must be at least 8 characters and not a common word. Type it again in Confirm Password.
    • Enter the teacher's First Name, Last Name and Email Address. Phone Number is optional.
    • Leave Role as Teacher.

  3. Click Add Staff. EduPulse shows a green message and the teacher appears in the staff list.

Give the teacher their login

EduPulse does not email the login details, so send them to the teacher yourself:

  • the website address: https://edupulse.perthartschool.com.au
  • their username and password

Ask the teacher to change their password after they first log in. They can do this from the menu under their name, then Change Password.

Teacher or Administrator? A Teacher can clock in and out and see only their own hours. An Administrator can see and change everyone's hours, staff details and settings. Only choose Administrator for office staff.
